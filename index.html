10 / OPERATIONAL DISCIPLINE

Limited Daily Batch Model

Up to 99 units per selected fresh batch to enforce quality, manage freshness, and control food waste.

Better Freshness Control: Baking in smaller batches ensures customers receive oven-fresh items.
Demand Testing: Helps measure real-time daily popularity across menu categories.
Waste Reduction: Minimizes unnecessary food waste and protects gross margins.
Controlled Production: Prevents inventory bloat and keeps kitchen staffing lean.
