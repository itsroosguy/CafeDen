CHAPTER /08 OPERATING ENGINE

Production Dispatch & Waste Management

Centralized hub-and-spoke production paired with batch drop scarcity to enforce <3% daily waste.

1. Central Bakery (Hub)

Heavy mixing, proofing, and baking done at 4:00 AM off-site central kitchen to minimize kiosk equipment and space cost.

2. Cold Transit Dispatch (5:30 AM)

Temperature-controlled morning dispatch delivers fresh batches to satellite kiosks prior to 6:00 AM tech commute rush.

3. Batch Drop Scarcity (<3% Waste)

Capping daily kiosk bakes to 300 units guarantees daily sellouts and keeps ingredient waste under 3%.
