RISK MANAGEMENT

Downside Risk Analysis & Mitigation

Proactively identifying key operational risks and executing clear risk-mitigation frameworks.

1. Footfall & Weather Volatility

Mitigation: Integration with Zomato/Swiggy delivery platforms + pre-booked B2B tech park corporate catering boxes during monsoon periods.

2. Raw Ingredient Inflation

Mitigation: Direct coffee estate sourcing agreements and fixed annual supplier contracts for butter and flour locking recipe COGS at 32%.

3. Lease Non-Renewal Risk

Mitigation: Sub-150 SQFT modular glass kiosks are 100% relocatable to a alternative tech park site within 48 hours.

4. Staff Turnover & Training

Mitigation: Standardized 3-day barista and assembler operating SOPs ensuring fast staff onboarding and zero quality drop.
