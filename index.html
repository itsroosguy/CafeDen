SLIDE /11 OPERATIONAL DISCIPLINE

Limited Daily Batch Model

Up to 99 units per selected fresh batch to manage freshness, understand demand, and control food waste.

Better Freshness: Baking in smaller batches ensures customers receive oven-fresh items.
Demand Testing: Measures real-time daily popularity across menu categories.
Lower Overproduction: Controls kitchen prep and prevents inventory bloat.
Better Waste Management: Reduces unnecessary food waste and protects margins.
